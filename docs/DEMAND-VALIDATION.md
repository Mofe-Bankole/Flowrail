# Demand validation — who to ask, what to ask, what counts

> **Post-submission.** Written 2026-11-17, after the Crypto World's Fair deadline. Not part of the
> judged entry (tag `submission-2026-10-12`). This is the plan for evidence we can show if
> Colosseum follows up, and for deciding whether FlowRail is worth building past the hackathon.

## The hypothesis we are testing

> Teams that pay **10+ people per month in stablecoins** either hold one over-powered hot wallet or
> spend real hours signing payouts one by one — and they would pay to approve a run once and have
> the chain enforce the rest.

Three things can be false, and we want to find out which:

1. **The pain** — maybe they do not pay that many people on-chain, or the current way is fine.
2. **The fear** — maybe nobody worries about the hot wallet until after they lose it.
3. **The switch** — maybe they already use Rise, Toku, Request Finance or Deel and are happy.

A conversation that kills one of these is a good conversation. Write it down either way.

## The interview — 15 minutes, no pitch until the end

Ask about the last real payout, not about opinions. People are accurate about last Tuesday and
optimistic about the future.

1. Walk me through the last time you paid a group of people. How many? How much in total?
2. What did you pay them with — bank, stablecoin, a platform? Why that?
3. Who approved it, and how many times did someone have to sign or click?
4. How long did the whole run take, start to last person paid?
5. Has a payout ever gone wrong — wrong address, wrong amount, a key or account compromised?
6. Where does the money sit before it goes out? Who can move it?
7. If you use Rise / Toku / Request / Deel: what do you wish it did differently?
8. *(Only now, show the desk or the 2-minute demo.)* Would approving the run once, and having the
   chain refuse anything outside each payee's key, change anything for you?
9. Would you run a real payout through it on testnet with us? *(The real test.)*

Do **not** ask "would you use this?" Everyone says yes. Question 9 is the only answer that counts.

## What counts as evidence

| Strength | Signal | Example of how to report it |
|---|---|---|
| Weak | "Sounds useful" | Do not report. |
| Medium | Describes the exact pain with numbers | "Agency A pays 25 creators/month in USDC from one shared wallet; 3 people have its key." |
| Strong | Agrees to a testnet pilot with their real payee list | "Agency B ran a 12-payee test run on Moderato on 2026-11-28." |
| Strongest | Written LOI, or asks about price unprompted | "Agency C: 'We'd pay per run if it settles on mainnet.'" |

Target for the next three weeks (before winners are announced ~2026-12-05):
**15 conversations, 5 with numbers, 2 pilots.**
